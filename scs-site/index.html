What We Do

Low voltage systems built for performance.

From backbone infrastructure to the devices your team uses every day, SCS installs the systems that move information through modern commercial environments.

01

Structured Cabling

Professional voice and data infrastructure for offices, commercial buildings and new construction.

  • Cat6 / Cat6A copper
  • Voice, data and coax
  • Wireless access point cabling
  • Testing and certification
02

Fiber Optics

High-performance fiber backbone installation, termination and splicing for scalable network infrastructure.

  • Single-mode and multimode fiber
  • High-count backbone cabling
  • Fiber splicing and termination
  • Patch panels and testing
03

Data Center Infrastructure

Deployment support for mission-critical environments where organization, documentation and execution matter.

  • Rack installation and rack & stack
  • End-to-end patching
  • Grounding and bonding
  • Network gear installation
  • Migration support and colo builds
04

Commercial Audio / Video

Integrated conference room and collaboration systems that make meetings simple and dependable.

  • Conference cameras and PTZ systems
  • Ceiling and wall microphones
  • Speakers and audio systems
  • Displays, video walls and equipment racks
05

Security Infrastructure

Low-voltage infrastructure and device installation for small commercial security deployments.

  • Access control
  • Camera systems
  • DVR / NVR systems
  • Supporting cabling and pathways
